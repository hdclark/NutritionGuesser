# Nutrition data provenance

The bundled catalog contains representative nutrient values normalized to **100 grams** and rounded for approachable game play.

Primary source:

> U.S. Department of Agriculture, Agricultural Research Service, Beltsville Human Nutrition Research Center. FoodData Central, primarily FNDDS 2021-2023 and SR Legacy.

FoodData Central data are public domain and published under CC0 1.0. Source site: https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/ and downloadable datasets: https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/download-datasets/.

These values are intended for a light educational comparison game, not medical advice or meal tracking. Foods and recipes vary by preparation, brand, serving size, and database release.
